Purchases and World Hosting

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This page describes optional purchases and managed world hosting in Tiny Block, provided by HARD TAB LLC under the No Such Games studio name. The price, currency, billing period, and availability shown by your app store before confirmation apply to your purchase. Paid features are available only when offered in the game and supported by your store.

1. Optional permanent purchases

A cosmetic pack unlocks the items listed in that pack. Glow unlocks an effect that lights the environment around your character. The Demolitionist skill unlocks crafting TNT and lighters; other players can still use items they obtain. These are one-time purchases, with no additional charge for using an unlocked feature again. They do not expire on a monthly schedule.

Purchases are associated with the store account and the game profile used to buy them. Use Restore purchases with the same store account after reinstalling the game. An App Store purchase does not automatically become a Google Play purchase, or vice versa. A refunded or revoked purchase may no longer provide access to its paid features.

2. Monthly managed world hosting

One hosting subscription provides one saved managed world for up to 16 players at a time, including its owner. The owner can make the world public or share a private invite code. Friends do not need their own hosting subscription to join. The owner's device does not need to remain online.

An empty world sleeps to avoid unnecessary resource use and starts when a player joins. Starting may take a short time. Hosting requires internet access; maintenance or technical faults can temporarily interrupt play. Local peer-to-peer worlds remain a separate free feature with their existing player limits and requirement that the host stays online.

3. Renewal and cancellation

Hosting is billed monthly and renews automatically unless you cancel through the store that manages the subscription. Your store displays the exact price and renewal terms before you confirm. Cancel before the renewal deadline shown by that store to avoid the next charge. Deleting Tiny Block, leaving a world, or resetting a world does not cancel the subscription.

After cancellation, hosting normally remains available until the end of the paid period shown by your store. Once paid access ends, players cannot join the managed world until hosting access is restored. A store-confirmed billing grace period can extend access; an unsuccessful payment alone does not.

4. Saved worlds and owner reset

Managed world saves are stored on our servers. After hosting expires, the saved world is retained for at least 90 days so the owner can renew and recover it. After that period it may be deleted. A cancelled subscription does not export a managed world to your device.

Only the owner can request a full reset. Reset creates a fresh world and removes the current world's progress, including progress made by its players. The game asks the owner to confirm the world name before proceeding and creates a server backup first. A reset does not remove global purchases or cancel the subscription. Backups are a recovery measure, not an in-game undo feature; contact support promptly if a reset was accidental.

5. Store support and refunds

Apple or Google processes payments. We do not receive your full payment card details. Purchase and refund rules depend on the store and applicable law. For an incorrect charge or refund request, use the store that charged you. For missing in-game access after a successful payment, restore purchases or contact us. Please do not send card details, passwords, or full purchase tokens.

Purchases require permission from the account holder. Younger players should ask a parent or guardian before making a purchase.

6. Platform terms and privacy

The App Store and Google Play terms also apply to purchases made through those stores. The iOS app uses Apple's Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement. How we handle game and purchase information is described in our Privacy Policy.
